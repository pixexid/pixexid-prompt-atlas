```text
Create a new wide 16:9 editorial campaign photograph from five supplied references. Image 1 is Mara's identity reference: preserve her recognizable oval face, warm medium-brown skin, dark brown eyes, short dense black curls, natural facial proportions and realistic skin texture, while changing her pose, expression, clothing and setting. Image 2 is Tavi's character reference: preserve the friendly teal otter's round ears, dark oval eyes, cream muzzle and belly patch, mustard neckerchief, short paws, rounded feet and one broad tapering tail. Image 3 is the product reference: preserve the coral cylindrical bottle, rounded shoulder, charcoal screw cap and exactly one integrated open carry loop. Image 4 is the authoritative FOLD & FIELD mark: preserve the continuous leaf with forest green upper-left and terracotta lower-right meeting directly with no white seam, plus the exact uppercase wordmark "FOLD & FIELD". Image 5 supplies only a material and palette language: tactile cream, apricot and forest-green folded paper, bold geometric planes and hard warm sunlight. Do not copy its portrait crop, bottle placement, canyon layout or object-by-object arrangement.

Stage an entirely new life-size paper-art pavilion in a bright public conservatory. Make a low, wide environmental composition photographed at human eye level. Mara is full-body on the left third, kneeling on one knee and leaning forward with an open joyful smile as she ties the loose end of Tavi's mustard scarf into place. Dress her in a practical cream canvas overshirt with rolled sleeves, forest-green trousers and terracotta sneakers. Preserve her identity rather than turning her into a generic model. Her hands must make clear, anatomically believable contact with the scarf.

Tavi stands on the center-right as a waist-high sculptural companion built from layered teal and cream paper, with visible folded facets and softly deckled edges. Translate the original illustration into a dimensional sculpture while preserving its simple proportions, facial warmth, cream markings and single tail. Give Tavi a delighted expression and a slight forward lean toward Mara; keep both feet structurally supported on the pavilion floor. No extra paws, ears, tails or scarf knots.

Mara holds the coral bottle naturally by its single cap loop in her relaxed left hand near her knee. The bottle remains fully recognizable and unobstructed. Print the complete corrected FOLD & FIELD lockup once, modestly on the bottle body, projected around the cylinder without a sticker, white rectangle, mirrored leaf or misspelled letters. Do not place the logo anywhere else.

Build the pavilion from a few monumental folded-paper arches and low terraces in warm cream, pale apricot and forest green, arranged as a new sweeping spiral that frames Mara and Tavi without repeating Image 5. One broad skylight from upper left creates all cast shadows; soft ambient fill keeps faces, paper facets and bottle branding legible. Match contact shadows, scale and color spill across the human, sculpture, bottle and set. Keep the scene playful, tactile and physically coherent. No collage panels, duplicate subjects, floating objects, unrelated text, extra bottles, extra light sources or copied composition.
```
