Create a new 9:16 vertical editorial beauty campaign scene using the four ordered references. Preserve both products' geometry, materials, caps, logo forms, and readable Verdessa branding without duplicating either product. Compose this as an intimate lived moment rather than a catalog lineup, with shallow depth but both product identities still readable. No extra bottles, no floating objects, no invented label panels, no de-aged skin, no copied neutral reference clothing, and no contradictory light sources.
